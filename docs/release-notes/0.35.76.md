The submission check no longer freezes Revit on big models, and the field test no longer freezes Revit on a warning.

- The submission check works in steps of about a minute at most and says NOT COMPLETE until the last step finishes (bug 81).
- The chat can open a model by its path, also in a Revit window with nothing open. Workshared models always open detached and are never saved.
- The field test turns Revit's warnings into non-blocking ones while it runs, so a warning dialog can't hold it up (bug 79).
- The crash notice after a closed field test is a small corner hint, not a blocking window (bug 80).
- The berth status list is short enough for the chat (bug 78).
