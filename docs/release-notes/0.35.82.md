Lifeline keeps changes made while editing a floor outline or a group, also when Revit closes during the edit. The TritoRevit tab shows the version that runs.

- Revit gives add-ins no quiet moment while you are in Edit Sketch or Edit Group. Outline edits finished there were not written down, so they were lost if Revit closed during the edit. Now they are written within about 2 seconds anyway, in small slices. A replay also no longer deletes a sketch's lines one by one (bug 89, please check).
- The TritoRevit tab's first panel is titled "TritoRevit" with the version, and its Update button shows the version under "Update".
