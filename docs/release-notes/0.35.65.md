Each project has its own chat on your phone, named after your coordinator and the project. The central model, your local copy and a detached copy share one chat.

- Messages you write in a project's chat on the phone go to that project's chat or Autopilot.
- Settings > Chat > Phone chats can rename a project or join two names into one chat.
- The phone side needs the Iris update on your website (git pull and Reload on PythonAnywhere); until then everything lands in the one main phone chat as before.
