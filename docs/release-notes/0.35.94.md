Questions have their own tab, jobs no longer go round and round, and Lifeline warns you before work is lost.

- Jobs no longer go round and round: a check doesn't send a job back for something only you can decide or that another job changed. It asks you once instead (bug 108, please check).
- Questions now have their own tab: click Questions (n) under the message box to see only the questions, numbered. Tap an answer, pick Other to type one, or type "4 yes" in the box. An answered question leaves the list. Say "I don't understand" and it comes back, written again.
- Lifeline shows a small hint in the corner when the central model's server stops answering, when a lot of work is unsaved, or when the PC shows signs that often come before a crash (memory, disk, Revit's window handles).
- A new Lifeline option, off unless you turn it on, saves your local copy every so many minutes (5 to 240, 20 by default). It never synchronizes. Set it in the Lifeline window or in Settings > Lifeline.
